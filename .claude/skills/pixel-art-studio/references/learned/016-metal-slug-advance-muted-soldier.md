# Study: Metal Slug Advance (Walter) — chunky muted action sprite, lots of animation

- **source**: Spriters Resource "Walter - Metal Slug Advance" (GBA), 800px sheet, native-size page screenshot; grid lines removed · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: ~40px tall soldier frames, 114 sprites · **colors**: 13 per sprite, 51 total
- **subject**: full action set (idle, run, crouch, shoot, grenade, death)

## Measurements
- outline: dark coloured (black 1%), edge lum 0.19 vs 0.45
- inner lines 19%; 1px runs 67%; dithering 1.8%
- saturation 0.35 (muted khaki/olive/blue); light from the top; shadows hue-shift cool (-9°)

## Observations
- chunky proportions (big hands, boots, head) at ~40px
- reactions are exaggerated: recoil, squash on landing, big muzzle flashes as separate bright sprites
- effects (flashes, explosions) are the brightest, most saturated things on screen; characters stay muted

## Rules to apply
- Make effects/feedback (hits, coins, upgrades) the most saturated pixels; keep characters slightly muted so feedback pops
- Exaggerate reactions with squash/recoil frames — satisfying tap feedback in an idle game
- Shadows shift cool, highlights warm

## Do NOT copy
- Military theme or character designs (SNK IP)
