# Study: Strider Hiryu (Namco X Capcom) — hard cel fighter, 3/4 view

- **source**: Spriters Resource "Strider Hiryu - Namco X Capcom" (PS2), studied from a native-size page screenshot · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: 54 sprites, ~80–90px wide · **colors**: 25 per sprite, 28 for the whole character · **palette saved as**: not saved
- **subject**: action-RPG fighter sprites (stances, crouches, attacks) in a three-quarter view

## Measurements
- ramps: skin 7 steps red-brown → peach (shadows strongly warm/red); cloth 6 steps; wraps olive-grey 4 steps; red 3–4 steps
- outline: coloured, never black — darkest red-brown on skin/red, dark navy on cloth, dark olive on wraps
- inner lines: 31% of interior pixels are dark line work (muscle splits, fold strokes, wrap gaps)
- texture: 61% of colour runs are 1px; dithering 2%
- light: upper-left in every sprite

## Observations
- ~3.2 heads tall: big head + hair mass, oversized hands and feet
- folds are diagonal dark strokes with a light band beside them; big flat colour areas, little blending

## Rules to apply
- Whole character on one ~28-colour budget; skin shadows go warm red, not grey
- Outline each material in its own darkest ramp colour; never pure black
- Spend line work on form: muscle splits, fold strokes, gaps between wrap bands

- The character design (Capcom IP). Study technique only.
