# Study: Eastward (Wes) — modern pixel character with a tiny palette

- **source**: Spriters Resource "Wes - Eastward", native-size page screenshot; frame borders and labels removed · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: ~54x65px, 34 frames (idle, talk, laugh, shock, sleep) · **colors**: 11 per frame, **15 for the whole sheet**
- **subject**: an expressive NPC with many emotion animations

## Measurements
- outline: no black at all; dark coloured edges (edge lum 0.11 vs 0.53) — strong contrast without black
- inner lines 14%; 1px runs 41% (large flat shapes); dithering 0.3%
- saturation 0.53; warm palette (orange, red, olive, brown); cool shadow shift (-4.5°)

## Observations
- the whole character uses 15 colours across 34 frames — total palette discipline
- big rounded shapes, chunky body, small detailed face area where emotion happens
- emotion animations change the face and arms a lot while the body silhouette stays stable (cheap to animate, very expressive)

## Rules to apply
- Lock a character palette (≈15 colours) before animating and never add colours per frame
- Put expressiveness in face + hands; keep the body silhouette stable between emotion frames
- Get contrast from dark warm outlines instead of black
