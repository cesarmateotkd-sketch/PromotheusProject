# Study: Ragnarok Online Assassin (Eremes) — soft, muted isometric MMO sprite

- **source**: Divine Pride static sprite sheet npc/2416 (downloaded directly) · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: 126 body sprites ~40x70px + 18 separate head/headgear sprites · **colors**: 45 per body sprite, 65 for the body set; heads ~150 (soft hair blending)
- **subject**: full MMO class sprite set, 8 directions; heads and bodies are separate layers

## Measurements
- outline: soft coloured — edge lum 0.32 vs interior 0.44; maroon/plum, never black
- inner lines: 14% (form comes from tone, not lines)
- texture: 59% 1px runs; dithering 0.8%; no semi-alpha
- light: upper-left
- saturation: low/muted (dusty plum, mauve, lavender-grey) with gold trim as the only accent

## Observations
- ~2.8–3 heads tall: head+hair ~35px on a ~70px body; huge spiky hair of many thin strands with black strand accents; tiny face (2–3px eyes, pink cheek)
- a muted cloak/tabard is the main silhouette shape; wraps show light bands

## Rules to apply
- Separate head and body sprites if characters need many hair/headgear variants
- Soft outlines one step darker than the fill; 3–4 smooth tone steps; few inner lines
- Keep the palette muted and let one accent (gold) carry focus

## Do NOT copy
- Gravity's character art; the very high head colour count (hair blending) is costly to animate
