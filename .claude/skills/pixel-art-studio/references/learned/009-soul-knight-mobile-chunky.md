# Study: Soul Knight — chunky mobile pixel characters

- **source**: Soul Knight fandom wiki (Knight.gif, Wizard.gif via the MediaWiki file API) · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: 40x40 frames (wiki images are 6x upscales) · **colors**: 8 per sprite, 17 total
- **subject**: playable characters as shown in-game on phones

## Measurements
- outline: 100% black/near-black, 1px, all around
- inner lines 17%; 1px runs 64%; dithering 1%
- light from the top; saturation 0.40
- animation: 2-frame idle at 200ms, 1px bob

## Observations
- the character is mostly a head/helmet with a tiny body — maximum readability at phone size
- one big shape per character (helmet, hood) carries identity; eyes are single red pixels

## Rules to apply
- For sprites under ~48px on a phone: hard 1px black outline, ≤ 8–10 colours, one big identifying shape
- A 2-frame, 1px idle bob is enough to feel alive at this size

## Do NOT copy
- The extreme minimalism for large hero art — it reads as "small game" if used for close-up characters
