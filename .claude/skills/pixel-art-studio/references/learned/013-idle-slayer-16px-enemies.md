# Study: Idle Slayer — 16px enemies for a pixel idle game

- **source**: Idle Slayer fandom wiki (Bee animation frames 16px, native) + its Portals/Enemies infographic (looked at, not measured) · studied: 2026-11-15
- **true size**: 16x16 frames · **colors**: 10 per sprite
- **subject**: enemies that scroll past constantly in an idle runner

## Measurements (Bee frames)
- outline: 86% black, 1px
- inner lines 28%; 1px runs 69%; no dithering; strong top light
- saturation 0.66 (vivid)

## Observations
- enemies are 16px, chunky, high-contrast, each one colour family (orange worm, green slime, white yeti, red demon)
- backgrounds behind them are dark and desaturated so the vivid enemies pop
- portal art uses big soft gradients, characters stay hard-edged

## Rules to apply
- Constantly-moving idle units: 16–24px, black 1px outline, one dominant colour family each, vivid on a muted backdrop
- Make each enemy type identifiable by colour alone (players see them for hours)

## Do NOT copy
- The infographic's upscaled/gradient assets as pixel-art evidence
